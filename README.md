# Supply Chain -Transportation-Utilization-Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze vehicle capacity utilization across transporters, routes, truck types, and Manufacturing Location

The primary objective of this dashboard is to identify underutilized vehicle capacity, evaluate transporter and route performance, improve truck selection and shipment planning, and support better utilization of available transportation capacity.

---
## Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Overview Analysis
[Click to view Overview Dashboard](Dashboard-images/Overview-dashboard.png)

* **Business Objective:** Provides a high-level view of overall Vehicle Fill Rate, underutilization, transporter performance, truck type performance, and route-level utilization.
* **Key Observations:**
  * **Overall Utilization:** The overall Vehicle Fill Rate is **75.64%**, slightly above the defined **75% target**.
  * **Underutilization:** **47.04% of shipments** have a Vehicle Fill Rate below 75%, indicating significant unused vehicle capacity.
  * **Transporter Performance:** **Mahindra Logistics (84.08%)**, **Blue Dart Express (81.32%)**, and **Allcargo Logistics (81.24%)** are among the highest-performing transporters.
  * **Trend:** Vehicle utilization shows a steady improvement from 2024 to 2026.

### 2. Transporter Analysis
[Click to view Transporter Dashboard](Dashboard-images/Transporter-dashboard.png)

* **Business Objective:** Provides a detailed analysis of individual transporter performance and helps identify areas of underutilization.
* **Key Observations:**
  * **Transporter Performance:** Selected transporters can be evaluated based on Vehicle Fill Rate, Weight Fill Rate, Volume Fill Rate, and Underutilization Rate.
  * **Truck Type Analysis:** Helps identify which truck types are being utilized efficiently and which require improvement.
  * **Route Analysis:** Highlights routes with relatively lower Vehicle Fill Rates that may require further investigation.
  * **Shipment-Level Analysis:** Identifies individual shipments where Vehicle Fill Rate falls below the **75% target**.

---
### 3. Insights
* The overall Vehicle Fill Rate is 75.64%, which is slightly above the 75% target. The analysis also shows a steady improvement in vehicle utilization from 2024 to 2026.
* Although the overall fill rate is above the target, 47.04% of shipments have a Vehicle Fill Rate below 75%. indicating that a significant number of vehicles are dispatched with unused capacity.
* Mahindra Logistics, Blue Dart Express, and Allcargo Logistics have the highest Vehicle Fill Rates of 84.08%, 81.32%, and 81.24%, respectively. These transporters are utilizing vehicle capacity more efficiently compared to the others. However, the shipment-level analysis shows that some individual shipments are still below 75%, highlighting opportunities to improve utilization further.

### 4. Business Recommendations
* **Improve shipment consolidation:** Smaller shipments going to the same destination or along similar routes can be combined wherever possible. This will help utilize available vehicle capacity more effectively.
* **Select the appropriate truck size:** Select truck types according to shipment weight and volume to avoid assigning unnecessarily large vehicles.
* **Improve route planning and scheduling:** Routes with consistently low fill rates should be investigated. Better shipment planning, consolidation, and scheduling can help improve utilization on these routes.
* **Monitor underutilized shipments:** Shipments with fill rates below 75% should be regularly monitored to identify recurring patterns. This will help the company take timely action and prevent repeated underutilization.
